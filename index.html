Piet Modrian

Piet Mondrian was a Dutch Painter and art theoretician.

This painting consists of thick, black brushwork, defining the borders of colored rectangles. As the title suggests, the only colors used in it besides black and white are red, blue, and yellow.

1872 * Born in Amersfoot

1911 * Previously known as Pieter Cornelius Mondriann, he changed his name to be professionally known as Piet Mondrian.

1933 * Mondrian produced Lozenge Composition With Four Yellow Lines which was a simple painting that innovated thick, colored lines instead of black ones.

1942 * Mondrian completed Composition in Red, Blue, and Yellow after he moved from Manhattan to London.

1944 * Piet Mondrian passes away at 71 in New York.
